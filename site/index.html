Princeton Math Calendar

Department events with speakers and talk titles, refreshed daily from Princeton Mathematics.


Google Calendar: On a computer, choose Other calendars → + → From URL, paste the URL above, and click Add calendar.

Outlook: Choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web, paste the URL, name the calendar, and click Import.

Subscribe by URL to receive updates. Each calendar app controls when it refreshes. Remove your original Princeton subscription if you want to avoid duplicate events.
